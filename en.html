PATER Privacy Policy

Application: PATER (PATER-Mobile)
Effective date: 2026-08-18
Controller / contact: Freedom Academy — mail@freedomacademy.app
Public URL of this policy: https://freedomacademysl.github.io/PATER-Mobile-privacy/en.html

1. What PATER is and what it is for

PATER is a self-control and digital-wellbeing tool: you yourself choose which apps you want to block, and PATER holds that decision even in the moment of temptation. It is a commitment device that you voluntarily activate on yourself; its purpose is to help you keep a restriction you chose.

PATER includes a curated default list of apps that cannot be removed — that's the point. You choose to add your own apps; the default protections stay firm as the guarantee of your commitment. This list (the default set and your additions) lives only on your device.

2. What data is processed and how

All processing happens on your device. To show the block screen, PATER uses Android's Accessibility Service only to read in memory which app comes to the foreground, then immediately discards that information; it does not read your screen content. PATER operates no server, does not send your activity to any server, does not log or monitor it, does not inject or block ads, and nothing is collected, stored, shared, or leaves your device.

3. On-device processing only

All processing happens on your device. PATER runs locally: there is no backend, no user account, no cloud sync. No data leaves your phone because of PATER.

4. Nothing is collected or shared

PATER operates no server, does not send your activity to any server, does not log or monitor it, does not inject or block ads, and nothing is collected, stored, shared, or leaves your device. There is no collection of personal data, no data shared with third parties, no advertising and no tracking.

5. Sensitive permissions PATER uses

  • Accessibility Service: used only to read in memory which app comes to the foreground, then immediately discards that information; it does not read your screen content.

6. Your rights

Because PATER collects and stores no personal data, there is no data about you to request, correct, or delete from any server: it simply does not exist outside your device. The apps you added to your personal list live locally and disappear when you uninstall PATER.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the revised version at the same URL.
