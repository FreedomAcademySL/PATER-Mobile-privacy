PATER Privacy Policy

Application: PATER (PATER-Mobile)
Effective date: 2026-06-04
Controller / contact: Freedom Academy — freedom.academy.s.l@gmail.com
Public URL of this policy: https://freedomacademysl.github.io/PATER-Mobile-privacy/en.html

1. What PATER is and what it is for

PATER is a self-control and digital-wellbeing tool: you yourself choose which apps and sites you want to block, and PATER holds that decision even in the moment of temptation. It is a commitment device that you voluntarily activate on yourself; its purpose is to help you keep a restriction you chose.

PATER includes a curated default list of apps and sites that cannot be removed — that's the point. You choose to add your own apps and sites; the default protections stay firm as the guarantee of your commitment. This list (the default set and your additions) lives only on your device.

2. What data is processed and how

This section reproduces PATER's canonical data-handling description word for word. It is the authoritative text: every other surface (the Data Safety form, the VpnService declaration, the in-app disclosure dialog) must match it.

PATER is a self-control and digital-wellbeing tool: you yourself choose which apps and sites you want to block, and PATER holds that decision even in the moment of temptation. All processing happens on your device. To show the block screen, PATER uses Android's Accessibility Service only to read in memory which app comes to the foreground, then immediately discards that information; it does not read your screen content. To block websites, PATER creates a local, on-device VPN that inspects only DNS queries: the domains you blocked are rejected on the device (an NXDOMAIN response), and the rest of your traffic passes unchanged. PATER operates no server, does not send your traffic to any server, does not log or monitor it, does not inject or block ads, and nothing is collected, stored, shared, or leaves your device. DNS queries that are not blocked are resolved by your device's configured DNS or public resolvers (for example 8.8.8.8 or 1.1.1.1), exactly as normal browsing does — PATER does not collect or store those queries.

3. On-device processing only

All processing happens on your device. PATER runs locally: there is no backend, no user account, no cloud sync. No data leaves your phone because of PATER.

4. DNS query forwarding (important clarification)

DNS queries that are not blocked are resolved by your device's configured DNS or public resolvers (for example 8.8.8.8 or 1.1.1.1), exactly as normal browsing does — PATER does not collect or store those queries. PATER is not the recipient of those queries: it simply lets them continue their normal course to the resolver your device already has configured. Those third-party resolvers are governed by their own privacy policies, exactly as when you browse without PATER.

5. Nothing is collected or shared

PATER operates no server, does not send your traffic to any server, does not log or monitor it, does not inject or block ads, and nothing is collected, stored, shared, or leaves your device. There is no collection of personal data, no data shared with third parties, no advertising and no tracking.

6. Sensitive permissions PATER uses

  • Accessibility Service: used only to read in memory which app comes to the foreground, then immediately discards that information; it does not read your screen content.
  • Local VPN (VpnService): a local, on-device VPN that inspects only DNS queries; the domains you blocked are rejected on the device (an NXDOMAIN response) and the rest of your traffic passes unchanged.

7. Your rights

Because PATER collects and stores no personal data, there is no data about you to request, correct, or delete from any server: it simply does not exist outside your device. The apps and URLs you added to your personal list live locally and disappear when you uninstall PATER.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the revised version at the same URL. Any change to data handling is first reconciled against the canonical document (CANONICAL-DATA-HANDLING.md), which is the single source of truth.
